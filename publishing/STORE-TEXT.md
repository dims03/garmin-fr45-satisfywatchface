## Description (English)

SATISFY FR45 is a minimal watch face made for the Garmin Forerunner 45, with large time digits, a high-contrast white background and black text.

Features:
- Time in your device's 12-hour or 24-hour format.
- English weekday and date (DD.MM).
- Battery icon and percentage.
- Daily distance in kilometres, daily steps and total daily calories.
- Heart rate from the watch's latest valid history sample.
- A curved, dotted progress indicator for your daily step goal.
- Fixed SATISFY lettering.

Please note:
- Heart-rate history and screen updates may lag behind the native Health Stats display; this is not a live, second-by-second heart-rate view. A dash appears when the app cannot obtain a valid recent sample.
- DIST is total daily distance, not a running-session distance.
- Recovery time is unavailable to this watch face on Forerunner 45; RECOVERY displays -- H.
- A dot fill indicates step-goal progress, not a sunrise or sleep graph.

## Langkah pengunggahan

1. Buka https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/developer/upload dan masuk menggunakan akun Garmin.
2. Jika diminta, buat profil developer dengan nama publik serta kontak dukungan Anda sendiri.
3. Pilih SATISFY-FR45-1.0.0.iq dari folder ini.
4. Isi metadata, deskripsi, gambar serta pilihan distribusi pada formulir Garmin. Draf di atas dapat disalin.
5. Periksa seluruh pratinjau listing dan perangkat yang didukung sebelum mengirim untuk review.

Status: paket telah diunggah pengguna ke langkah Enter Details. Halaman menampilkan Status: Verified dan juga Signature check failed. Kombinasi pesan ini tercatat pada bug Garmin CIQQA-4716 untuk unggahan pertama dengan SDK 9.2.0. Belum dikirim untuk review atau dipublikasikan.
